# Media and brand assets

The production admin upload endpoint lives only in `apps/admin` and uses Vercel Blob. The shared `MediaAsset` table stores asset metadata and URLs. `src/lib/media.ts` resolves published assets and identifies where a URL is used before replacement or deletion. An upload alone does not place an image on a public page; a published product, collection, festival, setting, or Studio section must reference it.

The official brand logo is checked into `public/branding/fashion-feet-official.png` and rendered by `BrandLogo`. Two user-supplied past-exhibition photographs are checked into `public/exhibitions/` and rendered by `PastExhibitions`. These are authentic exhibition images; do not substitute them for product-specific catalogue photography.

The owner also supplied 21 women's footwear photographs on the Desktop, labelled `w daily`, `w fest`, and `w c`. Original copies are preserved in the ignored `intake/products/women/` folders; checked-in copies are in `public/catalogue/women/{daily-wear,festive,ceremonial}/`. The Desktop source files were removed after hash verification at the owner's request. `WomenCatalogueGallery` shows studio-style transparent-background derivatives from `public/catalogue/women/studio/` on `/women` and on the matching women's `/shop` views while there are no published products. The derivatives share a fixed square frame and warm neutral background; each preview links to its unedited source photograph so exact details remain available. Festive photo 7 is identical to Festive photo 5, so the gallery displays it once. Ceremonial photo 1 and Festive photo 1 are the same source photograph; the image is intentionally shown in both owner-labelled catalogues. Ceremonial photo 5 is a close-up in its source and remains labelled as a detail view. The galleries are photo previews, with no inferred product names, prices, materials, sizes, or availability.

Local SQLite development can use local uploads, but production must retain the Blob integration and its `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` on the admin Vercel project. Never commit upload credentials or user-uploaded media from the local `public/uploads/` directory.
