# Media and brand assets

The production admin upload endpoint lives only in `apps/admin` and uses Vercel Blob. The shared `MediaAsset` table stores asset metadata and URLs. `src/lib/media.ts` resolves published assets and identifies where a URL is used before replacement or deletion. An upload alone does not place an image on a public page; a published product, collection, festival, setting, or Studio section must reference it.

The official brand logo is checked into `public/branding/fashion-feet-official.png` and rendered by `BrandLogo`. Two user-supplied past-exhibition photographs are checked into `public/exhibitions/` and rendered by `PastExhibitions`. These are authentic exhibition images; do not substitute them for product-specific catalogue photography.

The owner also supplied 21 women's footwear photographs on the Desktop, labelled `w daily`, `w fest`, and `w c`. Original copies are preserved in the ignored `intake/products/women/` folders; checked-in copies are in `public/catalogue/women/{daily-wear,festive,ceremonial}/`. The Desktop source files were removed after hash verification at the owner's request.

`WomenCatalogueGallery` shows studio-style transparent-background derivatives from `public/catalogue/women/studio/` on `/women` and the matching women's `/shop` views while there are no published products. Daily Wear previews use `studio/daily-wear/`; Festive uses `studio/festive-v2/`; Ceremonial photos 02–04 use `studio/ceremonial-v2/`, while revised photos 01 and 05 use `studio/ceremonial-v3/`. Versioned paths invalidate image optimization caches. The previews share a fixed square frame and warm neutral background. Edited previews may differ in details from the source photos, so every preview links to its unedited original. Festive photo 7 is identical to Festive photo 5 and displays once. Ceremonial photo 1 and Festive photo 1 share one original but now have distinct preview arrangements. Ceremonial photo 5 is only a close-up in its source; its full-pair preview reconstructs missing areas and is explicitly labelled as such. Confirm its inferred details against a full original before using it as a product listing. No product names, prices, materials, sizes, or availability are inferred from these images.

Local SQLite development can use local uploads, but production must retain the Blob integration and its `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` on the admin Vercel project. Never commit upload credentials or user-uploaded media from the local `public/uploads/` directory.
